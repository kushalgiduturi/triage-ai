# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below does not replace it.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Respect that this project handles a sensitive domain: complaints about safety, harassment and personal hardship. Keep examples synthetic and respectful.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including putting real complaint data or personal details in issues, pull requests or test fixtures.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through a private message on GitHub ([@kushalgiduturi](https://github.com/kushalgiduturi)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do so.
